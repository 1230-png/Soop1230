## 1. 빈방 (Empty Room)

**Style Influence 65 / Weirdness 20**

**Style:**
```
Korean mood hip-hop, R&B hip-hop, singing rap, late night city vibe,
breathy male vocal, restrained emotion, clear diction, natural phrasing,
American English pronunciation on chorus,
warm guitar chords, soft trap hi-hats, warm rounded bass,
talking melody delivery, spoken-word cadence, close-mic intimate delivery,
empty room ambience, quiet melancholy, diary-style confessional,
target 78 BPM
```

[Verse 1]

[Final Chorus]
